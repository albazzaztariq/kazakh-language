Putting words and endings together

The big rule: the verb goes last

English says "I drink water"; Kazakh says "I water drink" (subject, object, verb). Describing words come before the noun they describe, and endings carry the grammar.

Patterns

PatternExampleEnglish
Subject + Object + VerbМен су ішемін.I water drink (= I drink water)
Question: add бе/ба/пе/па/ме/ма at the endСен студентсің бе?Are you a student?
Negative of "to be": емес + endingМен студент емеспін.I am not a student.
Place: add the locative suffixМен үйдемін.I am at home.
