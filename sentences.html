Putting words and endings together

The big rule: the verb goes last

English says "I drink water"; Kazakh says "I water drink" (subject, object, verb). Describing words come before the noun they describe, and endings carry the grammar. Below, the Word for word column shows the exact Kazakh order, with endings joined by hyphens.

Patterns

PatternExampleWord for wordEnglish
Subject + Object + VerbМен су ішемін.I water drink-II drink water.
Question: put a question particle [ма / ме / ба / бе / па / пе] after the word you are asking about; see how to pick it belowСен студентсің бе?You student-you-are [question]?Are you a student?
Negative of "to be": емес + endingМен студент емеспін.I student not-I-amI am not a student.
Place: add the locative ending (picked on the Suffixes page)Мен үйдемін.I home-at-I-amI am at home.

Questions, step 1: look at the last sound of the word you ask about

Write the question particle separately, after the word.

The word ends inQuestion particle starts withExample
a vowel, р, й or умбала → ма, бар → ма
қ, к, п, с, т, шпкітап → па, студент → пе
б, в, г, ғ, д, ж, з, л, м, н, ңбқыз → ба, студентсің → бе
