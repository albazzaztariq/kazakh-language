Suffixes

Kazakh builds meaning by adding endings to the end of a word

How suffixes work

Kazakh uses vowel harmony: a suffix has a back-vowel form (а, ы, о, ұ) and a front-vowel form (е, і, ө, ү, ә), and you pick the one that matches the last vowel of the word. Consonants also change the suffix's first letter, as the tables show. Forms in the tables are the standard ones; a few words have irregular changes, so check new words in a dictionary.

Seven cases

Example word: үй (house). The right form depends on the final sound of the word.

CaseSuffixExampleMeaning
Nominative(none)үйhouse

Dative (to)-ға/-ге, -қа/-кеүйгеto the house

"I am…" endings

Kazakh has no word for "am / are / is" in the present: an ending on the noun does the job.
